    Legend:
    • Weekday target: how many people Autofill aims to put on this shift on a weekday
    • Weekday min: the lowest headcount this shift is allowed to drop to on a weekday, but only when it's marked Flex
    • Weekend target: how many people Autofill aims to put on this shift on a weekend; weekends are always strict, so this number is also the weekend minimum
    • Flex: lets this shift type sag from its Weekday target down to its Weekday min on a quiet weekday
    • Keep Apart: a keep-apart pair is never put on this shift together, even on weekdays
    • Min rest (hrs): minimum gap required between the end of one shift and the start of the next one for the same person
