    Shift types

    How many people each shift needs on weekdays and weekends

    NameStartEndWeekday targetWeekday minWeekendFlexKeep apart
    What the columns mean
    • Weekday target: how many people Auto Plot aims to put on this shift on a weekday
    • Weekday min: the lowest headcount allowed on a weekday, only when the shift is Flex
    • Weekend: headcount on a weekend. Weekends are strict, so it is also the weekend minimum
    • Flex: lets the shift drop from its weekday target to its weekday minimum on a quiet weekday
    • Keep apart: a keep-apart pair is never put on this shift together, even on weekdays
    • Minimum rest: the gap required between the end of one shift and the start of the next for the same person

    Keep apart

    A pair never shares a weekend shift, or a shift marked Keep apart. Sharing another weekday shift is allowed but flagged
